Who we are

History & Company Information

From our first muster to today's parade ground, 2nd Kuching Company has trained generations of boys in discipline, faith and friendship.

2nd Kuching Company is a local company of The Boys' Brigade, meeting weekly for drill, games, Bible class and badge instruction. Members progress from Junior Section through to Company Section, taking on NCO and officer responsibilities as they grow.

Our anniversary each year brings together current members, officers and old boys to mark another year of "Sure and Stedfast" service to Kuching.
